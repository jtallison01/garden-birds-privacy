Garden Birds — Privacy Policy Effective date: 19 September 2026 Contact: gardenbirds80@gmail.com 1. Who this policy is from Garden Birds is developed and published by Jordan Allison, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. In data-protection terms this means I am the “data controller” for anything described below. I am registered with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office as a data controller, registration reference ZC252976. Garden Birds has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising. The only thing it can send anywhere is play statistics, tied to a random number for your install rather than to you, and only if you say yes when the game asks — see section 4. Say no, or say nothing, and the game never connects to the internet during play. 2. Data stored on your device Your progress — your Seed balance, your feeders and nest boxes, and which birds you have spotted — is saved only on your own device, in the app’s private storage. It never leaves the device and I cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes this data. There is also a reset option in the game’s settings panel. 3. Photos you take in the game The in-game camera composes a picture of your garden and saves it into your device’s own photo library, in an album named “Garden Birds”. That copy is stored on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere, and I have no access to it. Because it is saved to your photo library rather than to the app’s private storage, other apps on your device that you have granted access to your photos may be able to see it, in the same way as any other picture in your library. If you choose to share a picture, the share is handled entirely by your device’s own sharing tools and by whichever app you send it to. This policy does not cover what those apps then do with it — their own privacy policies apply. 4. Play statistics (optional) Once, near the start, the game asks whether you would like to help improve it by sharing statistics about how it is played. Nothing is sent until you answer, and nothing is ever sent if you say no. You can change your answer at any time in the game’s settings panel (“Usage stats”). If you say yes, the game sends events such as which stage of the opening you reached, when you first photograph a bird, how many species you have spotted, what you spend Seed on, and how long you were away between sessions. These go to GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS, Copenhagen), which processes them on my behalf and shows me aggregate charts. Alongside each event the GameAnalytics software includes: a random identifier generated on your device for this install (not your advertising ID, which is switched off, and not tied to you or to any account), a session identifier, your device model and manufacturer, your operating system version, your connection type (wi-fi or mobile), and the game’s version. GameAnalytics’ servers, like any web server, also see the IP address the request came from and use it to work out the country for its dashboards; the game does not send your location, and the IP address is not visible to me. Nothing you type is sent — there is nothing to type into Garden Birds — and no names, email addresses, contacts or photos are ever sent. Because the random identifier lets one install be told apart from another, UK data-protection law treats these statistics as personal data even though they do not say who you are, and this policy treats them that way too. The lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in settings; switching it off stops any further sending immediately. GameAnalytics acts as my data processor: it handles the statistics only on my instructions. Some of the services it relies on are based outside the UK, so the data may be processed outside the UK under the safeguards GameAnalytics describes. It keeps the data under its data retention and deletion policy, after which it is deleted or made anonymous. GameAnalytics’ privacy and security information is in its Trust Center. 5. Children Garden Birds is suitable for a general audience but is targeted at users aged 13 and over. The optional statistics in section 4 are the same for everyone who says yes, and nothing else is collected from anyone, of any age. 6. Your rights Under UK GDPR you have rights to access, correct and erase personal data held about you, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent. The only personal data Garden Birds involves is the optional statistics in section 4. They are keyed to a random identifier that I cannot connect to your name, so to act on a request I will need your help to find your install — roughly when you played is usually enough. Contact me at the address above and I will respond within one month. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. 7. Changes to this policy If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Material changes affecting what data is collected will also be noted in the app’s release notes. 8. Contact gardenbirds80@gmail.com Garden Birds is an independent project.
