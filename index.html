Garden Birds — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 September 2026
Contact: gardenbirds80@gmail.com

1. Who this policy is from

Garden Birds is developed and published by Jordan Allison, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. In data-protection terms this means I am the “data controller” for anything described below.

Garden Birds does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising. The game does not connect to the internet during play.

2. Data stored on your device

Your progress — your Seed balance, your feeders and nest boxes, and which birds you have spotted — is saved only on your own device, in the app’s private storage. It never leaves the device and I cannot see it.

Deleting the app deletes this data. There is also a reset option in the game’s settings panel.

3. Photos you take in the game

The in-game camera composes a picture of your garden and saves it into your device’s own photo library, in an album named “Garden Birds”. That copy is stored on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere, and I have no access to it.

Because it is saved to your photo library rather than to the app’s private storage, other apps on your device that you have granted access to your photos may be able to see it, in the same way as any other picture in your library.

If you choose to share a picture, the share is handled entirely by your device’s own sharing tools and by whichever app you send it to. This policy does not cover what those apps then do with it — their own privacy policies apply.

4. Children

Garden Birds is suitable for a general audience but is targeted at users aged 13 and over. I do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, of any age, because I do not collect personal data at all.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights of access, correction, erasure, and complaint regarding personal data held about you. Because Garden Birds holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for me to disclose, correct, or erase — but if you believe otherwise, contact me at the address above and I will respond within one month.

You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Material changes affecting what data is collected will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
